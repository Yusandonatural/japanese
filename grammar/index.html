Five principles

Level 1 — four endings, nothing else

Most courses spend weeks on verb forms before you can say anything. Tag Grammar postpones all of that. At Level 1 the core has just four endings — ます, ますか, ません, ましょう — and time words do the rest. Learn these and start talking.
