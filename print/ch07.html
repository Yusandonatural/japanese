Easy Japanese 14 Days — Chapter 7 Workbook

Fill in the tag (は が を に で と へ から まで も) for the marked chunk, or write the Japanese for each English sentence. Answers on the last page.

1.きのうわたしもどうりょうのいえ[___]いきます。Yesterday I also went to my coworker's house.

6.こんしゅうつま[___]よるにへやでほんをよみます。This week my wife is also reading a book in her room at night.

8.あなたはあしたなにできょうと[___]いきますか？How are you getting to Kyoto tomorrow?

28.きょうだれがこども[___]びょういんにいきますか？Who is going to the hospital with the child today?
